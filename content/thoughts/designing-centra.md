So I decided to build something that doesn't try to impress me. Not another dashboard. Not another platform. Just a tool, something that does one thing well and then gets out of the way.

## What is Centra?

Centra is a minimal headless CMS written in Go. It stores your content and gives it back via API. No dashboards, no proprietary modeling, no content platform narrative.

## The invisible tool

The best tools disappear. Your content already has structure: files, folders, and formats. Centra serves it quietly and predictably, and it is boring on purpose.

## The trade-off

There is no real-time collaboration, visual editor, or workflow engine. Centra is built for a simpler loop: write, commit, deploy, fetch.
